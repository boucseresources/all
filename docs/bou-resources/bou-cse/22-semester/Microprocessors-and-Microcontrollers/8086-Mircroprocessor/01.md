**What is a microcomputer?**  
A microcomputer is a computer system consisting of a microprocessor as its CPU, memory, and input/output devices interconnected through buses.

**What are the functions of the CPU?**  
The CPU controls the activities of the system. It fetches instructions from memory, decodes them, and executes the required operations.

**Differentiate between the 8086 and 8088 microprocessors.**  
Both the 8086 and 8088 are 16-bit microprocessors. However, the 8086 has a 16-bit external data bus, whereas the 8088 has an 8-bit external data bus.
