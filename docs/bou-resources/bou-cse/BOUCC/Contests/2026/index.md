Registration Procedure
Team Composition
- Each team must consist of 3 students.
- Each team must have a Coach, who must be a faculty member of the respective university.
Online Registration
- Registration Opens: 2 October 2026
- Registration Deadline: 20 October 2026
- Registration Fee: Tk. 500 per team
Register Here:
ICPC Global Registration Portal
Competition Process
Online Preliminary Contest:
📅 27 October 2026
All accepted teams must participate in the online preliminary contest to qualify for the onsite regional contest.
Selected Teams Announcement:
📅 10 November 2026
Onsite Regional Contest:
📅 18–19 December 2026
📍 IUBAT—International University of Business Agriculture and Technology, Dhaka
Onsite Registration Fee: Tk. 8,000 per selected team.
