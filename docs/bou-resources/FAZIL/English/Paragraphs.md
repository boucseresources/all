??? "July Movement"

    The July Movement of 2024 in Bangladesh was a major student-led protest that began over the reintroduction of the quota system in government jobs. Students demanded a fair and merit-based recruitment process, arguing that the existing system was unjust and discriminatory. The movement quickly spread across the country, with thousands of students from various universities and colleges joining together. As the protests grew, clashes occurred between students and law enforcement, leading to casualties and nationwide unrest. The government imposed curfews and suspended internet services to control the situation. Despite the violence, the movement became a symbol of youth unity and courage. It reflected the determination of the new generation to stand for justice, equality, and good governance in Bangladesh.

??? "Unfairmeans in the Examination (পরীক্ষায় অসদুপায়)"

    Unfairmeans in the examination indicates a means or method which is very indecent. Nowadays most of the students adopt unfairmeans in order to pass an examination. It means that they secretly commit an act of copying answers into the answer scripts from materials in their possession. The purpose of an examination is two-fold. Through an examination the students acquisition of knowledge during a certain period of study is tested. Besides, it enables a student to give an account of how much knowledge he has gathered from his prescribed course. But by adopting unfairmeans a student frustrates the very purpose of the examination. He commits an act of crime by having recourse to a dishonest method. Besides, he cheats himself by seeking to prove that he knows things that he does not know and has copied from incriminating material. One who passes the examination by adopting unfairmeans remains ignorant and fails to come out successful in practical life. Resorting to dishonest means in examination has now created such a gigantic problem in our country that all conscious people are greatly alarmed at it. This criminal and self-deceiving practice of copying should be stopped immediately at any cost; otherwise, the nation as a whole will miserably fail to get through its struggle for existence in not too distant a future.

    **অনুবাদ :** পরীক্ষায় অসদুপায় বলতে এমন একটি উপায় বা পদ্ধতি বোঝায় যা অত্যন্ত গর্হিত। আজকাল পরীক্ষায় পাস করার জন্য অধিকাংশ ছাত্রই অসদুপায় অবলম্বন করে থাকে। একথার অর্থ এই যে, তারা নিজেদের সঙ্গে রক্ষিত বস্তু থেকে উত্তরপত্রে প্রশ্নের উত্তরগুলো নকল করে দেয়ার একটি কাজ গোপনে নিষ্পন্ন করে। বস্তুত পরীক্ষার উদ্দেশ্য থাকে দ্বিবিধ। পড়াশুনার নির্ধারিত কোনো সময়ের মধ্যে ছাত্রটি কী পরিমাণ জ্ঞান আহরণ করেছে পরীক্ষার মাধ্যমে তা যাচাই করা হয়। এছাড়া ছাত্রটি তার জন্য নির্ধারিত পাঠক্রম থেকে কতটুকু শিখেছে এভাবে তার একটি প্রমাণ দিতে পারে। কিন্তু অসদুপায় অবলম্বন করে ছাত্ররা পরীক্ষার উদ্দেশ্যটিই ব্যর্থ করে দেয়। প্রকৃতপক্ষে, অসৎ পন্থা অবলম্বন করে সে একটি অপরাধ সংঘটিত করে। এছাড়া, যা সে জানে না তা অপরাধমূলক বস্তু থেকে নকল করে দিয়ে জানে বলে প্রমাণ করার প্রচেষ্টায় নিজেকে প্রতারণা করে। অসদুপায় অবলম্বন করে যে পরীক্ষায় পাস করে সে অজ্ঞই থেকে যায় এবং বাস্তব জীবনে সাফল্যের মুখ দেখতে ব্যর্থ হয়। পরীক্ষায় অসৎ পদ্ধতির আশ্রয় গ্রহণ আজকাল আমাদের দেশে অত্যন্ত ব্যাপক এক সমস্যার সৃষ্টি করেছে যার জন্য প্রতিটি সচেতন মানুষই গভীরভাবে শঙ্কিত। এই অপরাধমূলক এবং আত্ম-প্রবঞ্চক প্রক্রিয়াটি অবিলম্বে যে কোনো মূল্যে দমন করা উচিত। নতুবা সমগ্র জাতি অচিরেই অস্তিত্বের সংগ্রামে টিকে থাকতে শোচনীয়ভাবে ব্যর্থ হবে।

??? "A Visit to a Place of Historical Interest"

    Last month I visited Lalbagh Kella, a famous historical place in Dhaka. It is an unfinished Mughal fort built in the seventeenth century by Prince Muhammad Azam, the son of Emperor Aurangzeb. Later, Nawab Shaista Khan continued the construction, but it was never completed. The fort stands on the bank of the Buriganga River and covers a large area with beautiful gardens, gateways, and old buildings. Inside the fort, there are several important structures, including the tomb of Pari Bibi, the daughter of Shaista Khan, the mosque, and the Diwan-i-Aam. The walls and designs of the fort show the rich art and architecture of the Mughal period. Visiting Lalbagh Kella was a wonderful experience for me. It helped me learn about our glorious past and made me feel proud of the history and heritage of Bangladesh.

??? "Good Governance"

Good governance refers to the proper and responsible management of a country’s social, economic, and political affairs for the benefit of all citizens. It ensures that power and authority are used transparently and that every decision made by the government serves the people’s welfare. The main principles of good governance include accountability, participation, transparency, rule of law, equality, and efficiency. In a system of good governance, leaders are answerable to the people, corruption is minimized, and justice is ensured for everyone regardless of class or background. It allows citizens to take part in decision-making and encourages freedom of speech and expression. Good governance also ensures that public resources are used properly and that services such as education, healthcare, and justice reach every citizen. When a government follows these principles, it builds trust, stability, and peace in society. Therefore, good governance is essential for the progress, prosperity, and sustainable development of a nation like Bangladesh.
